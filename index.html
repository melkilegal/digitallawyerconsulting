Legal Services for the Digital Economy

Data Protection and DPO Services

I provide data protection services under the Data Privacy Act (Republic Act No. 10173) and National Privacy Commission (NPC) requirements, including outsourced Data Protection Officer (DPO) services, training and support for DPOs and privacy teams, individual compliance documents, and development of data protection frameworks.

Work includes privacy policies and notices, privacy impact assessments, records of processing activities, data sharing and processing agreements, and review of the privacy implications of AI and other technologies.

My data protection experience started in 2018 at the International Organization for Migration (IOM), the United Nations migration agency, where I received institutional and GDPR training.

Engagements can cover a single document, support for an existing privacy function, or an outsourced DPO arrangement.

Digital, Technology & AI Legal Services

Businesses that adopt new technologies take on contractual and regulatory obligations around software, data, and AI.

Core services include SaaS agreements, software licensing, IT services and outsourcing agreements, API agreements, data processing agreements, and data sharing agreements.

AI-related services include AI use policies, responsible AI governance frameworks, vendor and procurement agreements, AI-specific contractual clauses, data and privacy review, confidentiality provisions, and risk and compliance documentation.

I have completed training in the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the EU Artificial Intelligence Act. This training supports my work on data protection and AI compliance documentation for clients whose operations involve EU data or markets. I am admitted to practice in the Philippines only.

International Organization & NGO Contracts Support

International organizations and NGOs operate within complex contractual, donor, regulatory, and operational environments. Contracts often need to accommodate institutional policies, donor requirements, government regulations, and the practical realities of program implementation.

I previously served as National Legal Officer in the Contract Law Division of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), the United Nations migration agency. I provide contract support to international organizations and NGOs.

Support includes contract drafting and review, negotiation, amendments, donor and funding agreements, project implementation agreements, service and vendor contracts, leases, construction and procurement-related agreements, and other operational contracts.

Work focuses on identifying legal and operational risks, clarifying contractual obligations, and preparing agreements suited to program delivery.

Whether you need support with a single agreement, a specific contractual issue, or ongoing contract review and advisory work, engagements can be structured around the organization’s needs.
